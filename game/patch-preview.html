Mission patch preview

The new patch designs, the award animation and the sound. Nothing here touches the game: the game files use the same three modules (patch-art.js, patch-fx.js, patches.js).

1. The award moment

Three lengths, like Breath of the Wild's pickup fanfares: a short one for ordinary patches, a longer one for rarer ones and the longest for the big milestones. Turn your sound on. The patch flies to the Patches button, top right.

2. The patches

Five shapes for five kinds of patch, with a stitched border in a different thread colour for each. Tap one to hear its note (every patch has its own, so the whole set is a small instrument).

3. Close up

Past about 90 px a patch also shows its number at the top and its name stitched round the bottom, like a real crew patch.

4. Not earned yet

Unearned patches show as an empty slot in their own shape (like the holes in a Pokémon badge case), so you can see how many are left and what shape they are.

5. Hear the three tiers

Rendered straight from the same code, in case you want to listen without the animation.
